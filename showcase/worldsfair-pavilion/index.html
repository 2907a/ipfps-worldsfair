What your app gets

One console, the whole platform. Capabilities below are running in production — roadmap items are labelled.

S2S API

17 endpoints

Users, balances (hold / settle / release / credit), score, referrals, products, purchases — HMAC-signed, app-pinned.

Solana escrow

Per-app SOL and USDC deposit addresses with token policies and on-chain settlement.

Webhooks v1

user.registered, deposit.confirmed, referral.linked — HMAC-signed envelopes pushed to your endpoint.

Shop digital goods

Product catalogue and purchase ledger shared across the network, exposed over S2S.

TG bots per-app

Bring your own bot token: Telegram auth and notifications scoped to your app only.

Referral graph

Core-owned referral edges with source attribution. Rewards execute in your own backend — the graph stays neutral.

Live feed per-app

Opt-out live feed ring, streamed to your users in real time over Edge WS.

Edge WS

Realtime balances, chat and live feed terminated at the network edge, fanned out per app.

EVM · Base · Arbitrum · TON · Tempo Roadmap

Ticketing Private domain

QR ticketing runs as a private domain today. Self-serve onboarding is on the roadmap.
